C++ project, replicated combat, chat transport, dedicated-server target, world-event and boss foundations, CI preflight.

Original hero, traversal, weapon set, 3 enemy archetypes, Hummer, helicopter pressure, Command Mech, cinematic intro/outro.

8 players, parties/squads, EOS auth/lobbies/sessions, reconnect, persistence prototype, moderation foundations.

## S3 — Open World Proof
World Partition streaming, traffic/civilians, day/night, weather, live events, safehouse, HLOD/performance budgets.

## S4 — Systems Depth
Inventory, mastery, classes, progression, faction reputation, raids, PvPvE and vehicle warfare.

## S5 — City Expansion
Riyadh and Casablanca only after Jeddah meets performance, retention and production-cost gates.

## S6 — Cloud/Web
Pixel Streaming 2 browser portal, autoscaling GPU sessions, device/touch QA.
