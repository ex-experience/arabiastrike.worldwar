# 07 — CAMPAIGN, MISSIONS & STAGES

## Three-act conflict structure

### ACT I — HOMEFRONT
Jeddah defense:
Corniche, Al-Balad, Downtown, Highway, Port, evacuation and local command.

### ACT II — THE NETWORK
The attack is revealed as part of a larger system:
research facility, cyber/network nodes, offshore operations, naval intelligence and command infrastructure.

### ACT III — WORLD WAR
Conflict expands across:
ground, sea, air, network/satellite layers and strategic command systems.

The Command Mech is part of escalation, not random spectacle.

---

## Canonical mission families from visual references

### 1. JEDDAH PORT ASSAULT
Infantry + boats + cranes + containers + wet dock + smoke + extraction.
Gameplay: port entry, squad clearance, boat interaction, vertical cranes, hostile support, extraction.

### 2. COMMAND MECH REVEAL
Industrial hangar infiltration culminating in gradual scale reveal.
Reveal order: feet → legs → torso systems → weapons → sensors → full body.

### 3. ROOFTOP RECON
Reconnaissance, route selection, scanner/network observation, rooftop traversal.

### 4. HIGHWAY CONVOY WAR
Mounted-gun combat, convoy formation, hostile drones, seat switching, moving vehicle combat, debris and branching support/pursuit choices.

### 5. CIVILIAN EVACUATION
Protect and route civilians under pressure.
ROE and civilian outcomes affect score/intel/story.

### 6. NIGHT URBAN PUSH
Rain, damaged traffic, emergency lighting, close urban movement and security response.

### 7. CORNICHE GUNSHIP ATTACK
Coastal infantry under helicopter pressure, air-defense objectives, sea impacts, rotor wash and destruction.

### 8. AL-BALAD EXTRACTION
Close-quarter heritage-district clearance/escort with tight alleys and authentic urban art.

### 9. MARITIME BREACH
Damaged flooded ship/offshore interior with red emergency lighting, electrical failure, tight CQB and changing water state.

### 10. ROOFTOP INSERTION
Rappel, zipline, drone overwatch, communications towers, coordinated rooftop assault.

### 11. GLOBAL WAR COMMAND
Interactive strategic map, theater status, fleets, aircraft, drones, network nodes and mission selection.

### 12. NAVAL ASSAULT
Helicopter insertion toward a fictional carrier/platform, deck/hangar combat, sabotage or extraction.

### 13. SCIENTIFIC FACILITY RESCUE
Identify civilians, secure scientists, avoid crossfire, recover data and prevent loss of critical research.

### 14. OFFSHORE PLATFORM DESTRUCTION
Approach, damaged platform reveal, emergency landing/infiltration, rescue, critical-system objective and collapse escape.

### 15. NIGHT MARITIME INFILTRATION
RHIB approach, engine-off final insertion, boarding, stealth clearance, radar/network disable and reinforcement escape.

### 16. CIVILIAN CRISIS PERSPECTIVE
Non-combat or low-combat moments showing citizens, traffic, communication failure and the human cost of escalation.

## Vertical slice narrative

Recommended 20–30 minute showcase:
`Command Briefing → Rooftop Insertion → Al-Balad Combat → Civilian Evacuation → Vehicle Transition → Corniche Engagement → Port Extraction → Mech Tease`

This slice must demonstrate:
infantry, squad AI/co-op, civilians, vehicles, traversal, sea, helicopter pressure, destruction, lighting transitions, cinematics and command/network identity.
