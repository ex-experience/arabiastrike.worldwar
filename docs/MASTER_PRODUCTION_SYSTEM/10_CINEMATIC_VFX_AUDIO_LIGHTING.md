# 10 — CINEMATICS, VFX, AUDIO & LIGHTING

## Cinematic camera languages

Command:
35–50 mm style, restrained movement, faces and decision-making.

Infantry:
24–35 mm style, controlled handheld energy, limited shake.

Vehicles:
24–50 mm style, chase and vehicle-mounted perspectives.

Scale:
18–24 mm style, coast, skyline, aircraft, naval/offshore structures, destruction.

Dialogue:
50–85 mm style to avoid making every conversation look like a trailer.

## Hero introduction concept
Black → radio chatter → JEDDAH COMMAND boot → red threat nodes → hero reveal from behind → command table → mission intel → 3D Jeddah map → helicopter insertion → hand control to player.

## Lighting bible
- Blue Night: port / rooftop / security
- Warm War Sunset: Corniche / convoy / large battle
- Wet Urban Night: downtown / emergency
- Warm Interior / Old City: Al-Balad / civilian sequences
- Red Emergency: offshore/ship/network crisis
- Cool Strategic Command: network/command spaces with limited hostile red

## Explosion classes
Frag-type game blast, launcher, rocket, vehicle, fuel, building, electrical, drone, artillery, missile-like game events.
Each needs tuned combinations of flash, smoke, debris, shockwave cue, dust ring, sparks, embers and environmental response.

## Destruction
Use:
- hero Chaos destruction for signature events;
- state-swap destruction for mid-tier assets;
- decals/debris/VFX for background damage.

Damage must persist where mission/world design requires.

## Audio identity
Port: cranes, ships, waves, metal resonance, radios, distant battle.
Al-Balad: narrow-alley reflections, enclosed reverb, market/urban layers.
Highway: wind, engines, tires, mounted-weapon pressure.
Mech hangar: sub-bass machinery, hydraulics, structural metal.
Offshore: rain, ocean, structure flex, alarms, pumps, electrical failure.

Spatial mix, occlusion, reverb zones and dynamic range are core gameplay/readability systems.
