# 02 — CHARACTER BIBLE

## Canonical hero: HUSSAM — Task Force Leader

One master digital-human identity across key art, cinematics, gameplay, UI portraits and marketing.

### Visual lock
- Arab / Saudi visual identity
- athletic field-operator physique; not exaggerated bodybuilder
- short dense black curly hair
- clean low fade
- full groomed black beard
- warm bronze/olive skin
- dark eyes
- black wraparound tactical eyewear
- black tactical base layer
- black modular plate carrier
- black tactical pants
- combat boots
- tactical gloves
- metal watch
- minimal chain
- controlled, calm, decisive expression

### Face / material quality
Cinematic skin must support:
- pores and micro-normal;
- forehead compression;
- under-eye detail;
- lip hydration;
- beard follicle shadow;
- eye wetness / tear line;
- subtle peach fuzz;
- sweat;
- dust;
- rain/wetness;
- minor bruising/blood;
- progressive combat dirt.

Material states:
`CLEAN → HEAT/SWEAT → DUST → WET → COMBAT DIRT → MINOR BLOOD → HEAVY DAMAGE`

### Hero loadout looks
1. COMMAND
2. URBAN ASSAULT
3. NIGHT / STEALTH
4. DESERT
5. MARITIME
6. ENDGAME DAMAGED

Each is modular. Stats-only cosmetic swapping is not enough; equipment should visually respond to mission requirements.

### Hero movement signature
Hero Tactical Walk:
- weapon low ready;
- shoulders controlled;
- head slightly lowered;
- eyes scanning;
- minimal arm swing;
- controlled stride;
- stabilized upper body;
- believable equipment secondary motion;
- procedural weapon inertia.

Variants:
Clean, Alert, Combat, Injured, Exhausted, Rain, Heavy Gear.

## Strategic antagonist

Design direction:
- 45–55 visual age;
- controlled and intelligent;
- civilian / strategic authority, not field soldier;
- minimal dark wardrobe;
- calm speech and posture;
- red strategic/network visual environment;
- believes his actions are necessary rather than acting as a caricature.

Must be original and not based on a real person.

Visual contrast:
- Hero = physical, dust, rain, blood, streets, human consequence.
- Antagonist = glass, clean geometry, red data, abstraction, distance, control.

---

## Civilians

Population generation target:
- 40–60 head variants;
- 12 male body bases;
- 10 female body bases;
- varied hair, facial hair, headwear, upper/lower garments, footwear and accessories;
- target 250–500 visible combinations before repetition becomes obvious.

Wardrobe categories:
Saudi traditional, Saudi contemporary, business, streetwear, workers, delivery, security, tourists, families, office, market vendors, fishermen, port workers, engineers, medical personnel.

Cultural clothing must be researched and implemented accurately rather than treated as costume.
